Cadence 1.0 for iPhone

Cadence brings your sleep, workouts, heart signals and how you feel together, then suggests a sensible training day and always shows why. Its readiness, sleep, strain, stress and fitness scores are estimates worked out on your iPhone from your own data.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Cadence has no sign-in. Everything stays on your iPhone.
  • How do I connect Apple Health? Open Cadence and follow the welcome steps, or Settings → Apple Health data. To change what Cadence may read later, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps → Cadence.
  • Why is a score missing? Scores are skipped rather than guessed when the data isn't there. Tap the score to see what's missing. Most need a few nights of sleep and some resting heart rate first.
  • How do I connect Garmin? Settings → Garmin: tap Create key, sign in on Garmin Connect, create a Personal API key, copy it, and paste it back into Cadence. Garmin is optional; Cadence works fully with Apple Health alone.
  • Does Garmin send VO₂ max? Not through its Personal API. Read it in the Garmin Connect app and log it on Today → Cardio fitness → Log VO₂ max.
  • How do I delete my data? Settings → Privacy & your data → Delete everything. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored.
  • Is this medical advice? No. Cadence is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose, treat or clear you for exercise. If something feels wrong, stop and seek care.
