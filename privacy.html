Cadence Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026 (Cadence 1.0)

Cadence ("the app") brings your sleep, workouts, heart signals and daily check-ins together on your iPhone and suggests a sensible training day, always showing why. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your health data stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

No account, no servers

  • Cadence has no sign-up or sign-in. The developer runs no server for the app and receives none of your data.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs are built in. Cadence does not track you across other companies' apps or websites and has no advertising identifier.
  • Your health data is never sold, never shared with data brokers, and never used for advertising or marketing.

Apple Health

  • Connecting Apple Health is optional and needs your permission in Apple's own sheet. Cadence reads only the types you choose (for example workouts, sleep, steps, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, VO₂ max). You can change this at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.
  • Cadence only reads. It never writes to, changes or deletes anything in Apple Health.
  • The copies it reads are stored only on your iPhone, in a database encrypted with iOS Complete file protection (unreadable while the phone is locked) and excluded from iCloud and computer backups.
  • Apple Health data is never uploaded, never sent to the developer or any third party, and never stored in iCloud.

What you enter

Daily check-ins (how you feel, soreness, symptoms), goals, your week plan, VO₂ max values you type in, and your source choices are stored only on your iPhone, with the same protection as above.

Optional services you connect yourself

Cadence can read your own data from services you already use. Each is optional, read-only and off until you connect it. Requests go directly from your iPhone to that service; nothing passes through the developer.

  • Garmin Connect. You create your own Personal API key on Garmin's website and paste it into Cadence. The key is kept in the iPhone's Keychain (this device only) and is sent only to Garmin to read your activities and daily values. Cadence never sees your Garmin password. Delete the key in Settings → Garmin, or revoke it on Garmin Connect.
  • Google Health (Fitbit) and Strava, where the app offers them. You sign in on Google's or Strava's own page; Cadence receives a read-only access token kept in the Keychain. Disconnecting revokes the token and deletes the copies.

Data read from these services is stored on your iPhone exactly like Apple Health copies. What those companies do with your data is covered by their own privacy policies.

On-device AI (optional, off by default)

If you turn it on, Apple's on-device model drafts week plans and words Coach answers from a summary of your own numbers. It runs on your iPhone only; nothing is sent to a cloud AI service. Coach chats are not saved.

Notifications

If you allow them, Cadence schedules local reminders on your iPhone (for example, 14 days before your Garmin key expires). They are created on the device; no push service is used.

Export and deletion

  • Export: Settings → Privacy & your data → Export a copy creates a file that you choose where to send. Anyone with the file can read it.
  • Delete: Settings → Privacy & your data → Delete everything removes all data the app stored and disconnects connected services. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored. Neither changes Apple Health or the connected services themselves.

Not medical advice

Cadence's scores are estimates from your own data, compared only with your own usual. Cadence is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or clear you for exercise. If something feels wrong, stop and seek care.

Children

Cadence is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. Material changes are also noted in the app's release notes.
